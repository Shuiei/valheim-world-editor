Dungeons and live moves: a placed dungeon gets its biome's real entrance on the ground, generated dungeons vary far more and read like buildings, up to 16 levels, dungeon blueprints, Cut (Ctrl+X), and objects moved in live mode stay the same objects in the running game.

This release covers 1.37.4 to 1.43.0 (the versions in between were not published on their own). The WorldEditorBridge plugin changed in 1.40.0: update it on the server to get live moves; an older plugin keeps working as before.

## Added

- **A placed dungeon gets the entrance of its kind on the ground**: **Place in the world** puts the game's own entrance for the biome (a burial chamber's mound, a sunken crypt's door, a Frost Cave's mouth, an infested mine's, a Morgen hole) at the middle of the view, facing you, with the dungeon 5000 m above it where the game lays that entrance's own. Its door leads up and back out, as in the game. The Plains keep the portal pair.
- **Go to the dungeon and Go to the entrance**: the view stays at the entrance after placing; two buttons take it up into the dungeon and back down, and the Dungeon panel's **Go to entrance** does the same for any dungeon of the area.
- **Up to 16 levels** (was 4).
- **Dungeon blueprints**: switch **A dungeon** on when saving a blueprint in the Workshop (or in the Blueprints tab's Details). Pasted into a world, it goes up behind the biome's entrance where you click; a portal in it marks where players arrive.
- **Cut (Ctrl+X)**, beside Copy in the Area tool's Copy and paste and on the Select tool's selection: copies, then takes the originals out of the world as one undo step, so a paste moves a build instead of doubling it.
- **Moved objects stay the same objects in live mode**: an object moved or turned in the editor moves in the running game under its own id, so mods that track objects keep knowing it (a chest's label sign is no longer doubled, a chest keeps its contents). Undo after applying moves it back. Where a player has both places loaded it is made again as before, and the Apply live message says how many. Needs the 1.43.0 plugin.
- **Claude's `generate_dungeon`** places the entrance the same way and takes up to 16 levels.

## Changed

- **Generated dungeons vary far more**: shaped rooms (notched corners, apses, L shapes, necks), bent corridors, two to four furnishings per kind of room, lighting styles, marble and wooden floors, lofts, sunken arenas, vaults, furnished alcoves and niches, clusters of things that belong together, one-off hero pieces, corridor kinds and dead ends with caches, and each biome's own props, lights, chests and crafting stations, all from the game's own prefabs, measured.
- **Generated dungeons read more like buildings**: side rooms grow in wings of their kin (a kitchen by the feast hall, cells by the guard room), a room's purpose sets its light, each level down holds a fifth more of the top level's loot and foes, a corridor's niche may hide a foe, and dvergr doorways carry the dvergr arch.

## Fixed

- **Spikes and an orange wedge in the 3D view after a visit to the map**: the spawn-point and nest markers, the Dungeon tool's marks and the Mountain tool's preview no longer draw into the new OpenGL context's buffers.

## WorldEditorBridge

- `/objects` takes objects to move, each under its own ZDOID (or made again where a player has both places loaded); `/status` lists `"features":["move"]`. Mods' listeners get the moves in `AppliedChanges.Moved`.
