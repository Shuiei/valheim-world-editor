The documentation inside the editor, add-ons for mods, and a sweep of bugs: **Documentation** opens a Help window with
every page and picture, searchable and offline, and `F1` or a panel's **?** opens it at the tool in use; the docs are
rewritten for new players, with a first edit, task guides, a page per tool, troubleshooting and every shortcut. Support
for Expand World, Riverheim and Tunnelheim now comes as add-ons, their own downloads; mods can bring their own tools.
Fly goes through the ground into tunnels and cellars. About sixty bugs are fixed, among them saving after an undo and
a mountain removing what was in a dungeon above it.

This release covers 1.87.1 to 1.109.0 (the versions in between were not published on their own). The WorldEditorBridge
plugin changed: update it on your game and server.

## Added

- **The documentation in the editor.** **Documentation** on the start page opens a Help window beside the editor: every page with its pictures (click one to see it full size), a search through all of them, links between pages, Back and Forward. It works without internet and matches your version. `F1`, on any page, opens it at what you are using, and each panel has a small **?** at the top that opens its page.
- **Documentation rewritten for new players**: install and a first edit on a copy of a world, a first build in the Workshop, twenty-one guides by task (back up a world, edit a server live, restore an area from a backup, raise past 8 m, roads, rivers, caves, moving a building, a dungeon, a seed…), a page per tool, how things work, troubleshooting by symptom, questions, every shortcut and a glossary, with new pictures. See the [documentation](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/README.md).
- **Add-ons.** Editor support for a mod can come as an add-on of its own: unpack it into the addons folder next to the editor, and it is used whenever its mod is installed; Settings lists them, each with a switch. The first three: Expand World, Riverheim and Tunnelheim.
- **Riverheim worlds** are recognised, opened, drawn with your own copy of the mod, made new from the New world page and edited live (with the Riverheim add-on); with the Expand World mods too.
- **Tunnelheim's tunnels and cellars** are drawn as the game builds them (with the Tunnelheim add-on), and Fly goes through the ground to reach them.
- **Mods' own tools and data**: a mod's extension can add a tool to the rail with its own settings card, draw whole areas with the game's ground look, read its mod's settings, mark objects that only hold its data, and keep what its mod holds of a copied place with the copy.
- **Live changes keep the game's object**: an object whose data you change keeps its identity in the running game, so mods that track it keep working.
- **Clearer cards for worlds of world generation mods that cannot open**, saying what is missing.

## Changed

- **Expand World and Riverheim support are add-ons**, their own downloads beside the editor's: unpack each into the addons folder. Their switches move to Settings, under Add-ons; the Expand World folder setting is gone (add the BepInEx folder with the mods in Settings instead).
- **Fly goes through the ground.** In Fly (`F` twice), `C` takes the camera below the ground into what a mod digs under it; `Space` brings it back up.

## Fixed

- **Saving after an undo.** Undoing something you had saved and saving again now really takes it out of the world (or puts it back): it said "Nothing to save" and left the world as last saved. After a save, an undo is counted as what the next save does, offline and live, and Discard puts back saved steps you had undone.
- **Nothing lost in dungeons above.** The Mountain tool, scripts' `Ground.Mountain` and saving raised ground no longer remove trees and other things inside a dungeon 5000 m above them.
- **Copies land right**: trees and rocks copied with the Area tool stand on the ground where you paste them, and a moved tree stands on the ground, not on other trees.
- **Dungeons, Workshop, scripts and Claude**: the Dungeon panel knows the dungeon you just placed, its buttons are named the same everywhere and the generator shows only the settings that apply; Save blueprint warns about falling pieces even with Support check off; scripts move a ship as one; Claude's flatten, road and paint stay within the game's ±8 m and saving a blueprint keeps its description and tags.
- **The window**: the top bar fits narrower windows, panels no longer overlap or hide under the status bar, cut-off labels are shown whole, saved and unsaved zones on the map are told apart, and stale messages no longer linger.
- **Mods' objects**: what mods' tools add is kept by the game, a mod's object changed twice is updated in place live, and extensions read the ground exactly as the game does.
- **Expand World Size and the Deep North** match the game to the bit (altitude, water depth, stretched biomes, the Deep North's edge).
- About twenty-five texts that said something untrue are corrected.

## WorldEditorBridge

- Objects can take new data in place, keeping their id, when the editor changes them.
- A live zone reset that keeps buildings also keeps the objects mods mark as holding their data.
- Settings files the editor reads from the game's BepInEx config folder are found through that folder's own list of files, and a folder replaced by a link while it is read is left out.
