The editor now speaks your language: French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Thai and Filipino, with its help pages and their pictures translated too. Choose one under **Settings → Language**; a computer set to one of them shows it from the start.

This release also brings 1.120.0, which was not published on its own. The editor now looks like Valheim's own menus, and its tools are sorted out: what several tools did under different
names, keys and settings has one home each. Build works in a world as in the Workshop, the Area tool is the Region
tool, Shape, Mountain and Stamp once are one Landform tool, the four paint buttons are one Paint brush, and the same
keys lift and turn everywhere. The Path tool's Cave is gone: the Tunnelheim add-on digs real tunnels.

The WorldEditorBridge plugin is unchanged: the 1.114.5 plugin works with this editor.

## Changed

- **The editor looks like the game's own menus.** Near-black panels with a bronze line round them, squared corners, warm white text in the game's serif (carried in the program, so it looks the same on every computer) and orange titles, on every page: the start page, the map, the 3D editor, the Workshop, Settings and Help.
- **The Area tool is now the Region tool** (`B`). Its actions are sorted into four groups: **Ground** (Flatten, Raise, Lower, Smooth, Naturalize, Erode, Paint, Heightmap), **Objects**, **Recover** and **Clipboard**. See [Region](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/area.md).
- **Objects of a region go to the Select tool.** **Select the objects** takes what stands inside the box or polygon and opens Select with it selected: `Del` removes, **Replace the selection** turns it into another kind. The Region tool's own Remove objects and Replace objects are gone; the new **Only** list narrows the choice to one kind ("Beech1 (42)").
- **Recover gathers what brings a place back**: **Back to generated ground** (was "Restore the ground"), **Regrow nature**, **Back to a backup** and **Reset zones**. Reset zones has the same choices in the Region tool and on the map, with **Keep my buildings** on at first.
- **One Landform tool** (`G`) in place of Shape, Mountain and the brushes' Stamp once. Its **Kind** is **Formula** (mounds, craters, your own formulas), **Mountain** (peaks, ridges, volcanoes) or **Picture** (a greyscale picture pressed in). Every kind shows what a click will add before you click. See [Landform](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/landform.md).
- **One Paint brush in place of four.** One **Paint** button on the rail, with the four paints as swatches in its panel. The keys are the same: `6` Dirt, `7` Cultivate, `8` Paved, `9` Clear paint.
- **Place is for nature**: trees, rocks, bushes, crops, pickables, creatures and spawn points. The pieces of the hammer left its list for Build.
- **One blueprint library.** The **Blueprints** panel of a world and the Workshop's **Library** tab show the same list with the same buttons; **Edit** is now **Open**, and **New in Workshop** is **New blueprint**.
- **No limit is one switch, on the Mask card**, beside the panel of every tool it governs. It was in two places, and three tools followed it without showing it.
- **One Cut slider, for worlds too.** The slider in the status bar hides everything above a height in every 3D view, so you can look, select and build inside buildings in a world as in the Workshop. See [Cut](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/editor-basics.md#cut).
- **Go to a player is beside LIVE**, in the top bar, while a player is connected.
- **The same keys lift and turn everywhere.** `PgUp`, `PgDn` and `Ctrl` + wheel lift, `,` `.` and `Alt` + wheel turn, in Build, Select, Paste and Place. **`Shift` always makes the step finer** (in Select it used to make it coarser). Select and Paste turn by 22.5° and lift by 0.5 m at first; Place turns by 15°, and by 1° with `Shift`.
- **One key, one meaning.** `R` turns a quarter turn wherever something turns. Place's New layout moved from `R` to `N`, Paste's Mirror from `F` to `X` (`F` is Walk and Fly everywhere), and a Path ramp's end height from `Alt+Shift`+click to `Ctrl+Alt`+click.
- **One word, one meaning.** Place's **Zone** mode is **Polygon** ("zone" is only the game's 64 m zone), Select's Pick list is **Selects**, the rail's Natural is **Naturalize**, the brushes' Smooth falloff is **Soft**, and each **Clear (Esc)** says what it clears.
- **One action, one result, whichever tool applies it.** Raise, Lower, Flatten, Smooth, Naturalize, Restore and Paint are worked out in one place for the brushes, the Region tool and the Path tool. One **Smooth** along a path evens out a little more than before, and Naturalize's bumps may sit very slightly differently.
- **Heightmap's Export writes the selection**, not the whole open area (the whole area when nothing is selected).

## Added

- **The editor in French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Thai and Filipino.** Choose one under **Settings → Language**; a computer set to one of them (or to Portugal's Portuguese, or Tagalog) shows it from the start. Every text that comes from the language catalogue is translated; the few windows not moved to it yet stay in English. Corrections are welcome: see [Translating](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/translating.md).
- **The help in your language.** The Help window shows each page in the editor's language, with its pictures taken in the editor in that language, its contents and its search, so the help names each button as you see it. Messages the editor still shows in English are quoted in English, with a translation beside them. A page whose English has changed since it was translated says so, with a button to read the English page.
- **Build in a world.** A new **Build** tool on the rail (`U`) opens the Workshop's Build dock over a world's open ground: the game's pieces in their tabs with their pictures, the search, your mods' pieces, the cost, and **Auto**, **Manual** and **Free** snapping. See [Build](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/build.md).
- **Lay pieces along a line or in a grid.** At the Build dock's foot, **Lay** puts the picked piece end to end along a line, round a circle or a rectangle (several layers high), or in a grid for a floor, in a world and in the Workshop.
- **One building bar for Build, Select and Paste**: **Snap**, **Grid**, **Turn by**, **Lift** and **Built by**, with one setting for the three, kept for the next time.
- **The Path tool has Naturalize, Erode and Restore**: put natural bumps back on a flattened road, weather a ditch, or take a road back to the ground the game generated long after the undo history is gone.
- **Every action shows the settings it uses.** The Region and Path tools' Naturalize and Erode show their own bumps and rest angle; before, they silently used what was last set in the brushes.
- **A Rate for the Place brush**: how fast a drag plants is Place's own setting, no longer the sculpt brushes' Strength.
- **Save the clipboard as a blueprint from the Blueprints panel.** A **Clipboard** row shows what was copied last, with **Paste** and **Save as blueprint…**: a copy made with Select no longer needs the Region tool to become a blueprint.

## Removed

- **The Path tool's Cave** (and Claude's `dig_path` cave). It could only dig a trench and lay boulders over it, since the game's ground cannot overhang. Real tunnels, rooms, shafts and stairs are dug with the **Tunnel** tool of the [Tunnelheim add-on](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/addons/Tunnelheim/README.md), in worlds played with Tunnelheim. Caves already made stay as they are.
- **Stamp once** in the Raise and Lower brush panels: choose **Landform**, Kind **Picture**. A stamp as a brush shape still paints as before.

## For Claude

- `dig_path` takes `naturalize`, `erode` and `restore` too, and no longer `cave`.
- The cut is the control `view.cut` (it was `workshop.cut`), usable in a world, the Workshop and a dungeon.
