Two new add-ons and a History that stays: the **ValheimRAFT** add-on draws ships and land vehicles whole and moves,
copies and deletes them as one, live too; the **Railroads** add-on draws track, stations and trains, and what you place
with the editor works in the game as if built there. History is no longer emptied by saving, applying live, a zone
reset, reloading or discarding, and leaning building pieces keep their lean.

This release covers 1.109.1 to 1.112.1 (the versions in between were not published on their own). The WorldEditorBridge
plugin changed: update it on your game and server.

## Added

- **A ValheimRAFT add-on.** Ships and land vehicles built with ValheimRAFT are drawn whole, as the mod builds them, with their swivels, sails, ropes and rope ladders and what they carry, and move, copy and delete as one, live too. See [ValheimRAFT](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/addons/ValheimRAFT/README.md).
- **A Railroads add-on.** The track, stations and trains of the Railroads mod are drawn in the 3D view as the game builds them, from the mod's own models; track, trains and station signs you place, paste, move or turn with the editor work in the game as if built there, saved or live.
- **Trains and other tied objects stay together.** A mod's `worldeditor.json` can tie objects to one that names them (a locomotive and its wagons, a deck and what is built on it): selecting, moving, turning, copying and deleting take the whole group, and each pasted copy gets ids of its own.
- **For mod authors**: link rules can give the parts hanging on a parent their own offset and turn, or carry them where they were saved (a cart aboard a ship); extensions can turn the frame a parent's parts hang in, place a part themselves, read the ground's height in shape hooks, follow every object of their prefabs the editor places, moves or turns (`AddPlacer`), and set or take away a quaternion value in a copy. See [For mod authors](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/modding.md).

## Changed

- **History is kept whatever you save or apply.** Pasting a blueprint or a building, or shaping ground past the game's ±8 m, then saving or applying live, no longer empties History: undo it and save or apply again, and the ground comes back exactly as it was. Zone resets keep it too (only what the reset made again is left out), and it is all there when you open the world again.
- **Mods' objects an add-on draws show as drawn**: no grey stand-in box inside the drawing, picked and outlined by what is drawn, and shown in the Place and Paste previews.

## Fixed

- **Reload and Discard keep the History in a live world.** Every step the game has stays in History, applied and still undoable; only the steps not applied yet go, with their changes. Discard reads the world from the game when it has to, as it says it does.
- **Leaning building pieces keep their lean when a world is opened.** A pitched or rolled piece (as a blueprint built in the Workshop or a mod can place it) was read upright, and moving, turning or copying it then saved it upright. It now keeps its pitch and roll as the world stores them.

## WorldEditorBridge

- **Mods know at once what the editor pastes.** Objects the bridge makes, moves or changes go through the game's own handling of objects that come from another game, so a mod that finds objects by ids it keeps (ValheimRAFT's ships and ropes) finds pasted ones right away: a host's game no longer drops the ropes of a ship pasted live.
