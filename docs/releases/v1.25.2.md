Claude can now change what is already in a world, not only add to it: move, turn and swap objects, find places, weather the ground, dig rivers and caves, edit the game's dungeons and use every control of the editor. Also: areas up to 17 × 17 zones and much faster drawing of crowded areas, the game's locations drawn whole, spawn points you can place and see, Follow that grows the area, and Direct3D on Windows for the drivers that flickered.

This release covers 1.20.1 to 1.25.2 (the versions in between were not published on their own).

## Added

- **Claude changes what is there** (`move_objects`, `set_transform`, `replace_objects`, `get_object`, `set_object_data`, `copy_objects`): moves and turns groups of objects as one (a building keeps its shape), puts objects exactly, swaps their kind, reads and changes what they hold (a portal's tag, a ward's players, health…), and copies them in rows or rings.
- **Claude finds places and shapes ground**: `list_locations` and `search_world` find the start, traders, altars, dungeons and objects; `shape_ground`, `regrow_nature`, `dig_path` (rivers, moats, caves, ramps), `stamp`, `import_heightmap` and masks work the ground as the editor's tools do; `preview_seed` and `create_world` make new worlds.
- **Claude edits the game's dungeons**, saves and opens blueprints, puts blueprints into worlds, and uses **every control of the editor** (`list_controls`, `set_control`). Saving, applying live and touching your files stay refused. See [docs/claude.md](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/claude.md).
- **The game's locations are drawn whole**: a crypt entrance's rocks, the trader's camp, a boss's altar, a troll cave's walls show with **Ruins & structures** (the save keeps only a marker; the game makes these parts again at each load, so they cannot be picked).
- **Spawn points and creature nests** have their own kinds, say what they spawn (`Boar · 0–2 ★ · spawns once`), are found by creature in Place's search and show as coloured boxes with markers in 3D (off at first). Objects are sorted into real kinds: **Other objects** went from about 470 kinds to a handful.
- **A saved world's list keeps up to date** by itself, with a **Refresh** button.

## Changed

- **Larger areas**: 9 × 9, 13 × 13 or 17 × 17 zones (up to 1088 m across).
- **Much faster drawing of crowded areas**: only what is in view is drawn and small objects fade far away; a 17 × 17 area with 2.3 million objects went from 31 to about 75 frames a second.
- **Follow grows the area** instead of moving it, up to 16 zones each way, never leaving zones behind.
- **Windows draws through Direct3D**: with some drivers (older ones, laptops with two graphics chips) the 3D view and the map showed old frames and black lines (#115). **Settings › Drawing** can go back to the driver's OpenGL.
- **The tool rail and the Place panel** are in clear sections; Place's weight sliders are shares in % that always make 100%; **One at a time** places only the object at the cursor.
- **While the game's look is prepared**, the start page's actions are greyed out.

## Fixed

- **A ruin you built onto shows whole** (#115): a ruined tower patched with wood showed only the wood; a ruin's pieces touching yours now count as your building. The map has **Show ruins**.
- **Rocks that shape the ground** (the Plains' rock pillars, coast and forest boulders) were missing from the 3D view and lists.
- **Location markers** no longer put a post on every invisible No limit ground disc.
- **Moving objects on the Workshop's plot** lost what they held (a chest's contents, a sign's text).
- **Workshop**: a click that moved the mouse a little placed nothing.
- **Unsaved marks** kept their green dots after **Save to world**; areas with very many new objects or scripts on them were slow.
- **Previews over the sea** were hidden behind the water; kind names lost their underscore in the Place lists; **Discard** failed without a word when the world could not be read.
