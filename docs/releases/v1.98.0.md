Riverheim worlds, add-ons, and a much larger SDK for mod authors: worlds made with Riverheim open, draw, and can be made new and edited live, as the game makes them with your own copy of the mod; editor support for a mod can now come as an add-on of its own, unpacked into the new `addons` folder; and mods' editor extensions can draw whole areas with the game's ground look, add their own tools, keep their data with copies, and change objects live without the game making them again.

This release covers 1.87.1 to 1.98.0 (the versions in between were not published on their own). The WorldEditorBridge plugin changed: update it on servers and games you edit live.

## Added

- **Riverheim worlds.** With the Riverheim switch on in Settings and Riverheim installed, a world made with Riverheim opens and is drawn with your own copy of the mod: its ground, biomes, forests, map and predictions are Riverheim's, as the game makes them, with or without the Expand World mods. The worlds list tags Riverheim worlds, the New world page can make one, and a game or server running one opens live.
- **Add-ons.** Editor support for a mod can now come as an add-on of its own, made by anyone: unpack it into the new `addons` folder next to the editor (next to the app on macOS), and it is used whenever the mod it is for is installed. Settings lists the add-ons and whether each is in use. See [Add-ons](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/modding.md#add-ons).
- **More for mod authors' editor extensions** (see [Modding](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/modding.md)):
  - a mod's file can mark objects that only hold its data: the editor no longer shows them as boxes, and a zone reset that keeps buildings keeps them;
  - an extension can read the settings of the game's own scripts, as your copy of Valheim has them;
  - it can draw what its mod makes of an area (a canal, rock dug out of a hill) with the game's ground look, in place of the editor's ground, and clicks land on it;
  - it can add its own tool to the rail, under **Mods**, with a settings card, the pointer and keys, previews, and changes that go in the history like the editor's own;
  - it can keep what its mod holds of a copied place with the copy and its blueprint files, put back where you paste, turned and mirrored with it.
- **Changes keep the game's object live.** In live mode, an object whose data you change (in the inspector, or with a mod's tool) keeps its identity in the game instead of being removed and made again, so mods that track it, or that refuse to let their records be removed, keep working.

## Fixed

- **Expand World Size matches the game to the bit**: the altitude and water depth settings, "Stretch biomes" (one point in fifteen had another biome), and the ground along the Deep North's edge on wider or stretched worlds.
- **The Deep North's ground matches the game to the bit**, with or without Expand World.

## WorldEditorBridge

- Objects take new data in place, keeping their id, when the editor changes them.
- A live zone reset that keeps buildings also keeps the objects mods mark as holding their data.
