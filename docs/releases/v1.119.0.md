A new Workshop: the Build panel is now a dock along the bottom of the window with bigger piece pictures, a
Materials panel counts what your building costs, and building goes faster with Auto, Manual and Free snapping, drag to
lay a row, `Shift` + right click to take a piece away and a middle click to pick the piece under the pointer. Your
installed mods' pieces are listed and drawn in the Workshop without a live game. The Railroads add-on follows
Railroads 0.4.0's fitted track.

This release covers 1.115.0 to 1.119.0. The WorldEditorBridge plugin is unchanged: the 1.114.5 plugin works with this
editor.

## Changed

- **A roomier Workshop.** The Build panel is now a dock along the bottom of the window, so the plot keeps the rest: bigger piece pictures in rows, the station before each group, **Pieces**, **Mod pieces** and **Library** tabs, the search at the right. Drag its top edge to make it taller, or fold it down to its tabs; both are kept. The snapping, grid, turn and lift settings sit in a bar beside the tool rail, and the blueprints show side by side as cards. See [Workshop](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/workshop.md#the-build-dock).

## Added

- **Your installed mods' pieces in the Workshop, without a live game.** The **Mod pieces** tab reads the mods installed in your Valheim and mod manager profiles from their files: their pieces are listed under each mod with their pictures, drawn on the plot with their own models and snap like the game's. Their cost is unknown until a live game tells it. **Rescan mods** reads them again.
- **Materials panel.** The Workshop shows what the building costs at the right, as it changes: every material with how many, the stations needed nearby, the pieces with no known recipe (by mod) and those only the editor can place. **Materials** in the top bar closes or opens it.
- **Snap Auto, Manual or Free.** The building bar's **Snap** replaces the Snap to pieces switch: **Auto** as the game's hammer, **Free** without snapping, and **Manual**, where `Q` and `E` choose which snap point of the piece meets the others (marked on it), reaching further: for crowded corners where the hammer picks the wrong edge.
- **Fine placing with Alt.** Holding `Alt` while building places the piece by a quarter metre, without snapping.
- **Quick building.** Drag to lay a row of the piece, edge to edge; `Shift` + right click takes a piece away; a middle click builds with the kind of the piece under the pointer.
- **Recent pieces.** The dock's foot keeps the last 12 pieces you picked, newest first: click one to pick it again.
- **For mod authors: extensions can turn what they place** (`SetTurn` in the SDK). See [Mods and the editor](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/modding.md).

## Fixed

- **Alt in the Workshop takes effect at once.** Pressing or letting go of `Alt` without moving the mouse now changes the piece that follows it, and a click places what it shows; a piece placed with `Alt` on sloping ground stands on the ground where it goes.
- **The Materials panel counts what was built while it was hidden** (behind the Inspector), when it shows again.
- **One damaged mod no longer hides every mod's pieces** in the **Mod pieces** tab: it is left out, the others are listed.

## Mods

- **Railroads 0.4.0** (Railroads add-on 0.5.0): its new fitted track is drawn bent between its ends as the game builds it, trains placed on it stand on its line, it is listed in the Workshop under Track, and pasted or moved turned it keeps its shape. Railroads 0.3.7 and 0.3.8 change nothing the add-on reads.
- **Railroads** (Railroads add-on 0.4.0): its track, stations and trains are listed in the Workshop's **Mod pieces** tab by their names in the game, without a live game.
