Clicking and selecting in the 3D view: a click finds the object you see under the pointer, even under a tree's crown, a standing tree is outlined by its trunk, a huge selection is one box instead of an orange blob, the unsaved dot is much smaller, and the Area tool's Flatten, Raise and Lower can go past the game's ±8 m.

This release covers 1.37.0 to 1.37.3 (the versions in between were not published on their own). The WorldEditorBridge plugin is unchanged since 1.36.0 apart from its version number.

The Windows program carries an Authenticode signature (Certum). Each file comes with a Sigstore signature (`<file>.sigstore.json`); [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/SECURITY.md) says how to check a download.

## Added

- **No limit under the Area tool's Flatten, Raise and Lower**: the **No limit: past the game's ±8 m** switch shows with those three actions too (the same switch as at the bottom of the brush options), and the cut and fill line counts all of the move.

## Changed

- **A click finds an object by its drawn shape**: the boxes round objects only say which objects to try; their models' triangles decide, so a chest, a stone or anything under a tree's branches can be clicked. Leaves and bushes count only where nothing solid is behind them.
- **A standing tree is outlined by its trunk**, a column the tree's full height, instead of the box round its whole crown. Logs and stumps keep their box.
- **A large selection is marked by one box**: past 500 selected objects the 3D view draws one orange box round them all instead of one round each.
- **The green dot on each unsaved object is much smaller**: 5 pixels at 1080p, 9 at 4K, with a soft edge.
