The editor now knows modded games: it asks the running game what its mods add, draws their pieces with their own models and textures, and reads their data by name. Mod authors can tell it the rest in a `worldeditor.json`, ship an extension DLL built on the new SDK, and talk to the live bridge from their own plugin. Also: a pasted town stands as it was copied, glass is drawn see-through, the history survives opening another area, and the Windows program is signed.

This release covers 1.25.3 to 1.36.0 (the versions in between were not published on their own).

## Added

- **Modded games' objects**: opening a live world asks the game for the objects its mods add. They get their names, can be placed, the Workshop's Build panel lists them on a **Mods** tab by mod with their cost, and the inspector says which mod an object comes from. What was learned is kept, so the world read offline afterwards shows them too. See [Modded games](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/live-mode.md#modded-games).
- **Modded pieces drawn as in game**: a mod's pieces are drawn with their own models and textures, in the 3D view, the Workshop and the Build panel's pictures, found by name in the mods' own bundles from your Valheim and mod manager profiles. Crunched textures and Unity's cylinder, sphere and capsule are read too.
- **Mods' data by name in the inspector**: the values mods keep on objects show by their names instead of numbers, found in the mods' DLLs (never run).
- **Linked objects**: objects a mod builds out of others (a ship and what is built on it) are drawn where the mod builds them and moved, turned, copied and deleted whole. See [Linked objects](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/select.md#linked-objects-ships-and-other-mods-groups).
- **For mod authors**: an optional `worldeditor.json` (labels and help for the mod's data, prefabs not to offer, groups of pieces, how its objects link); **editor extensions** (a DLL built against the `ValheimWorldEditor.Sdk`, turned on in **Settings → Mod extensions**) that read the mod's packed data, rewrite ids in pasted copies, draw what the mod builds in code and leave the ground undrawn where the mod digs; and **live mode for mods' own plugins** (`WorldEditorBridge.Api.dll`): a plugin registers with the bridge, is told what the editor applied and answers routes of its own, and an extension can call them. See [For mod authors](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/modding.md).
- **The Windows program is signed** with the maintainer's code-signing certificate, so Windows names the publisher.

## Changed

- **WorldEditorBridge**: new `GET /catalogue` (every kind of object the game knows, mods' included), `POST /models` (what the game draws of some prefabs), `GET /mods` and `POST /mods/<id>/<name>` (mods' plugins registered with the bridge); every apply tells the registered plugins what changed. The log says more of each apply: objects before and after, peers, and a second later whether the destroyed objects are still there. Older editors see no difference.
- **Live mode opens a new connection after 10 seconds unused**, before the game closes the old one.

## Fixed

- **A pasted town stands as it was copied**: copy takes only the ground the buildings stand on, pasted objects keep what they hold (chests' contents, signs' text, builder and health), the ground goes past ±8 m when needed, **Clear the site** no longer digs around foundations, and a paste that does not fit the open area is refused with a red outline.
- **Opening another area erased the history made before it**: steps that do not fit the new area are kept aside and come back when their area is opened again.
- **Glass and other see-through materials are drawn see-through**: crystal windows and mods' glass show, no longer invisible or opaque. Mods' textures under other names than `_MainTex` are drawn; mods' sounds and effects are no longer offered in Place.
- **Worlds made with generated zones had no locations and no creatures**: the creator now spawns every location the seed lays out and each zone's control object.
- **Live mode could lose a request with "Connection reset by peer"**.
- **The Cut sliders start at their right end, with no cut.**
