Selection like Blender, and the Expand World mods built in full: a drag now selects what you see inside a box on the screen, with Blender's keys, an outline round the selected shapes and filters for built pieces or nature; New world, Regrow nature, the map and the Dungeon tool follow Expand World Data's locations, blueprints, vegetation, alt biomes, rooms and water colours as the mods make them; and moving a building keeps its shape.

This release covers 1.60.1 to 1.87.0 (the versions in between were not published on their own). The WorldEditorBridge plugin did not change since 1.60.0: keep the one you have.

## Added

- **Selecting like in Blender or a map editor.** A drag on empty ground (or `Alt` + drag over a building) draws a box on the screen and selects only what is seen inside it, so you can take one floor, one wall or a roof instead of a building from the ground up; **Select through** (`Alt` + `Z`) also takes what is hidden behind. `Ctrl` + click adds or takes out, `Shift` + click takes out, `Ctrl` + drag adds, `Shift` + drag takes out, and the row from the last object clicked is `Ctrl` + `Shift` + click. `Ctrl` + `A`, `Alt` + `A` and `Ctrl` + `I` select all, none or the rest; `Ctrl` + `H` hides the selection in the view to reach what it covers (`Alt` + `H` shows it again); `Shift` + `F` frames it. A second click at the same spot selects the next object behind. The Select panel's **Pick** takes only built pieces or only nature, and **A drag draws** brings back the zone on the ground. See [Select](https://github.com/Shuiei/valheim-world-editor/blob/main/docs/select.md).
- **Alt biomes where the game puts them.** Dark Meadows, Rock Black Forest, Goblin Plains and the rest are worked out from the seed: the New world page shows the dungeons they add or keep out, Regrow nature and New world grow what they add, the map names them under the pointer, and the 3D view draws an Expand World Data alt biome's own ground look.
- **New world builds the Expand World locations the mod changes**: objects added, swapped or given data, levelled or painted ground, random damage, blueprint locations (with their terrain snapshots) and cloned locations, as the mod spawns them, instead of leaving those zones to the game.
- **Expand World vegetation grows as the mod grows it**: rules with data, drops and blueprints, rules that want the greenest spots, and axis scales.
- **The Dungeon tool follows Expand World Data's room and dungeon files** (rooms turned off, changed or copied, themes, sizes, room counts and limits, doors, object swaps and data, blueprint rooms), and joins each room no nearer the entrance than the game allows.
- **The map shows more**: the start and traders with the game's icons (or the location file's), the sea at the world's real water level, and with Expand World Data the territory and status effects under the pointer.
- **Expand World Data's water colours** in the 3D view, with its "Custom water color" setting on.

## Changed

- **The selection is outlined, not boxed**: an orange line follows the selected objects' shape, seen through what is in front, the last one clicked in a lighter yellow; selected objects lose their unsaved green dot while selected.
- **Regrow nature and New world keep the ground clear round locations as the game does**, and follow the game's random draws more closely, so what grows after a village, a dungeon entrance, a chest or a fire comes out as the game makes it.

## Fixed

- **A building moved with "Put each object on the ground when moving" off keeps its shape**: the whole selection moves as one block, up or down with the ground under its middle, instead of coming out crooked on uneven ground.
- **Objects a mod hides deep under the ground are no longer picked** with what stands above them: moving a house with such crafting stations under it no longer lifts it 10 km.
- **Locations stand on the ground as the game spawns them** in the zones the editor generates, and so do the objects the game drops onto it (the start temple's stones, the tar pits' ribs, the traders...).
- **Expand World**: only the mods installed and on change a world; Expand World Data's location and alt biome files are used even when a world opened before the game's rules were read; ground the mod paints shows and edits; locations are predicted where Expand World Size puts them on bigger or stretched worlds; blueprint locations are laid out at the mod's size; odd files are refused with a message instead of stopping the editor; vegetation rules that need a global key follow Expand World Data 1.79's renamed fields.
