# Find a seed with what you want near the start

Let the editor look through many seeds and pick one whose start has the biomes, traders or bosses you want close by,
then make the world.

**You need:** the editor and Valheim on this computer. No world needs to be open.

1. On the start page, choose **A new world** under **Create**, then **Make a new world**.
2. Under **Find a seed**, keep **The start on a large landmass** and **Land all around the start** on, or turn them
   off if you like islands.
3. Optional: turn on **Each biome reachable on foot from the start** to avoid needing a boat early.
4. Set the distances you want, in metres: **Swamp within**, **Mountain within**, **Plains within**, a trader
   (**Haldor within**, **Bog Witch within**, **Hildir within**) or **Bosses 1–5 within**. Leave a box at 0 for no
   wish. Trader and boss wishes make the search a few times slower.
5. Choose how many seeds to look at: **60 seeds**, **150 seeds** or **400 seeds**.
6. Press **Find seeds**. The bar shows how far it is; **Stop** keeps the best found so far.
7. Under **Found seeds**, hover a seed for its facts, then click it to see it on the large map.
8. Check it on the large map: the search looks at each seed coarsely and may miss small islands or narrow straits.
   Optional: switch on the **Dungeons** kinds you care about.
9. Type the world's **Name**, choose **Saved in**, and press **Create world**.
10. Start Valheim and load the world once before you edit it in the editor: the game makes each place as players come
    near it.

![Find a seed: the wish list, then the best seeds found, each as a small map with its seed and score](../images/new-world-find.jpg)
*The wishes on the left; the best seeds under **Found seeds**, best first, each with its score.*

## Check it worked

- The editor opens the new world's map with the message "Made … (seed …)".
- In Valheim, the world is in the list of the folder you chose; a new character arrives at the red dot's place.
- The traders' and bosses' places are where the page showed them.

## Undo it

Nothing is made until **Create world**. To get rid of a world you made, delete its folder from the world folder it
was saved in (the **Saved in** choice). A seed you like can always be typed again: the same seed makes the same world.
