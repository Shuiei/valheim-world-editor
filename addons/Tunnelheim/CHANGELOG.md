# Changelog

Changes to the Tunnelheim add-on for the Valheim World Editor. Newest first. It goes out with every editor release,
as its own zip and under its own version.

## v0.1.0 — 2026-10-21

### Added
- **Tunnels are drawn.** A world's tunnels show in the editor's 3D view as the game builds them from Tunnelheim's
  records: the rock dug out, filled and painted, with the game's ground look, and the ground cut away over each
  column the mod rebuilt.
- **Tunnel records in words.** The inspector says what a tunnel record holds: its edits, its checkpoint and whether it
  keeps its ground.
