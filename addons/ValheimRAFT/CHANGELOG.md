# Changelog

Changes to the ValheimRAFT add-on for the Valheim World Editor. Newest first. It goes out with every editor release,
as its own zip and under its own version.

## v0.1.0 — 2026-10-23

### Added
- **Vehicles are drawn whole.** Each piece of a ValheimRAFT ship or land vehicle is drawn where the mod builds it on
  the vehicle, turned and tilted with it, the pieces on a swivel with the swivel; checked against the mod's own
  building on a server.
- **Vehicles move, copy and delete as one.** Any piece selected takes the whole vehicle; a pasted vehicle gets ids of
  its own, and its ropes, switches, sails and plants follow the copy.
- **Sails and ropes.** Custom sails are drawn between their corners in their colour, ropes from each rope anchor to
  what it is tied to.
- **The mod's values in words** in the inspector, and its pieces under the vehicle hammer's headings in the Build
  panel.
